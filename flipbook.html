About This Home
A Century of
Elegance

Step through the leaded glass doors and into over a century of history. This extraordinary Edwardian estate stands as one of Colorado Springs' finest surviving examples of early 20th-century residential architecture.

Soaring ceilings, hand-carved mahogany details, and original tile fireplaces tell the story of an era when craftsmanship was art. A top-to-bottom restoration brings it all into the present without sacrificing an ounce of soul.

The kitchen and primary suite have been reimagined for today's lifestyle — professional-grade appliances, heated floors, a spa bath — while the formal rooms remain breathtakingly intact.
